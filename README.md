top-ranked pattern: 39 stories across 6 regions, a world map, a KPI
numbers bar, a UTC timeline, a red-box DATE CHECK, three illustrated
leads and a region distribution bar. The leads are NATO's alarm over
Russia's nuclear-armed "Sputnik-S" satellite, ESA/JAXA BepiColombo
entering Mercury orbit after eight years, and the first foreign astronaut
aboard China's Tiangong station. Below them: the EU's 22nd Russia
sanctions package, Russia's own near-500,000 war toll, and 30 more
stories from every region.
